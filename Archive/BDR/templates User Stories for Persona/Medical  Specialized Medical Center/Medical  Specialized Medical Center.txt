Medical / Specialized Medical Center – User Stories
1️⃣ CEO / Medical Director – Dr. Hossam Fathy
#	User Story	Acceptance Criteria	Priority	Module / Feature
1	As a CEO, I want to view a real-time dashboard of all departments, so that I can monitor operations and make informed decisions quickly.	- Dashboard shows all department KPIs
- Updates in real-time (≤ 1 min delay)
- Alerts for anomalies (delayed tasks, billing issues)	High	Analytics & Reporting
2	As a CEO, I want to receive automatic alerts when key metrics drop below thresholds, so that I can take immediate action.	- Alerts configurable per KPI
- Delivered via Email, WhatsApp, Telegram, or in-app
- Alerts must include actionable details	High	Notifications & Analytics
3	As a CEO, I want to approve high-value transactions or invoices, so that I can ensure financial compliance.	- Can approve/reject invoices > threshold
- Audit trail of approvals
- Notifications to Finance module	High	Finance / Billing
4	As a CEO, I want to assign tasks or strategic initiatives to department heads, so that execution aligns with organizational goals.	- Assign tasks to departments
- Track completion status
- Dependencies visible
- Notification sent to assigned heads	High	Operations / Workflow
5	As a CEO, I want to generate historical performance reports, so that I can evaluate trends over months or years.	- Reports exportable (PDF, Excel)
- Filter by department, KPI, date range
- Can schedule automated reports	Medium	Analytics & Reporting
2️⃣ Department Head – Dr. Sara Adel
#	User Story	Acceptance Criteria	Priority	Module / Feature
1	As a Department Head, I want to see all appointments and tasks for my department, so that I can manage team workload efficiently.	- Calendar view of appointments
- Tasks linked to staff
- Drag-and-drop rescheduling
- Conflict alerts	High	Scheduling & Operations
2	As a Department Head, I want to review lab or imaging results as soon as they are ready, so that I can inform patients and doctors promptly.	- Automatic notification when results available
- Secure access to patient records
- Status tracking of each result	High	Medical Records & Notifications
3	As a Department Head, I want to track staff performance, so that I can identify bottlenecks or training needs.	- Visual dashboards of completed tasks
- Alerts on delayed assignments
- KPI scoring for each staff member	Medium	Analytics & Reporting
4	As a Department Head, I want to delegate urgent tasks automatically to available staff, so that operations are not delayed.	- System can reassign uncompleted tasks
- Notifications sent to reassigned staff
- Audit trail maintained	High	Operations / Workflow
5	As a Department Head, I want to access patient histories quickly, so that I can provide informed medical advice.	- Searchable patient records
- Filter by visit date, diagnosis, tests
- Fast retrieval (<2 sec)	High	Medical Records / CRM
