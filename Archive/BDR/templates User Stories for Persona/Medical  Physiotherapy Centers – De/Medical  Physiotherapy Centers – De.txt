Medical / Physiotherapy Center – User Stories
1️⃣ CEO / Owner – Ahmed El-Sayed
#	User Story	Acceptance Criteria	Priority	Module / Feature
1	As a CEO, I want to view real-time KPIs for patient visits, staff performance, and revenue, so that I can make informed strategic decisions.	1. Dashboard updates every 5 minutes.
2. KPIs include patient attendance, revenue, and staff efficiency.
3. Can filter by date range, department, or staff member.	High	Analytics & Reporting
2	As a CEO, I want to receive automatic alerts for any delayed sessions or patient complaints, so that I can intervene promptly.	1. Alerts sent via email, WhatsApp, and in-app notification.
2. Alerts categorized by severity.
3. History of alerts accessible for audit.	High	Notifications / Operations
3	As a CEO, I want to track department budgets and financial performance, so that I can optimize resource allocation.	1. Finance module shows department-wise revenue, costs, and profit.
2. Can export reports as PDF or Excel.
3. Alerts if spending exceeds budget.	High	Finance / Accounting
4	As a CEO, I want to analyze patient satisfaction trends over time, so that I can improve services.	1. Collects survey results from Customer Support.
2. Generates trend graphs.
3. Can segment by treatment type, therapist, or department.	Medium	Customer Support / Analytics
