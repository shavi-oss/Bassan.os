2. CRITICAL FINDINGS SUMMARY
2.1 Over-Engineering Issues
Current State: Architecture Board Re-Submission proposes 32 containers (12 Day-1 + 20 Phase-2)

Problem:

32 containers exceed MVP capacity by 80% (recommended ≤40 for enterprise MVP)
Microservices complexity will slow initial delivery
Operational overhead exceeds team capacity in first 12 months
Recommendation:

Day-1: Reduce to 12 containers maximum (already proposed)
Phase-1 (Months 7-12): Add 8 containers maximum
Phase-2 (Months 13-18): Add remaining 12 containers
Total MVP: 20 containers by Month 12 (within acceptable range)
2.2 Security & Compliance Gaps
Critical Missing:

No explicit compliance mapping to regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, SOC2, ISO27001)
Disaster Recovery plan lacks quantitative RTO/RPO definitions
Multi-tenancy isolation lacks penetration testing requirements
Security policy service delayed to Phase-2 (risk for regulated markets)
No data retention/archival policy defined
Recommendation:

Conduct DPIA (Data Privacy Impact Assessment) before Day-1 development
Define RTO/RPO: Critical services (RTO<1hr, RPO<15min), Non-critical (RTO<4hr, RPO<1hr)
Implement Security Policy Service in Phase-1 (Month 7-9) instead of Phase-2
Define data retention: Transactional data (7 years), Audit logs (10 years), Documents (as per contract)
2.3 Operational Gaps
Critical Missing:

No incident response playbooks defined
No capacity planning model
No cost management strategy
No service level objectives (SLOs) defined
No deployment pipeline design documented
No monitoring & alerting strategy defined
Readiness Status: NOT READY FOR DEVELOPMENT
