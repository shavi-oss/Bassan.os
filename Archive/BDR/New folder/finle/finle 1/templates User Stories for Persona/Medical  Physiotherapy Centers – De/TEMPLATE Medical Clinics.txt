SECTION 2: User Stories (Detailed & Mapped)
🧑‍⚕️ Persona: Clinic Owner / Medical Director
Story 1: Clinic Performance Overview

As a Clinic Owner, I want to view a real-time dashboard of clinic performance, so that I can make informed business decisions.

Acceptance Criteria

Dashboard shows daily, weekly, monthly revenue

Revenue broken down by doctor and service

Patient volume trends displayed

Accessible only to owner role

Priority: High
Module: Analytics & Reporting

Story 2: Doctor Performance Monitoring

As a Clinic Owner, I want to monitor doctor productivity and revenue contribution, so that compensation and scheduling are optimized.

Acceptance Criteria

Number of patients per doctor

Revenue per doctor

No-show rate per doctor

Exportable reports

Priority: High
Module: Doctors & Staff Management

Story 3: Compliance & Audit Visibility

As a Clinic Owner, I want to track access logs to medical records, so that patient data privacy is ensured.

Acceptance Criteria

Log shows who accessed each medical record

Timestamp and action recorded

Non-editable audit trail

Priority: High
Module: Operations & Compliance

🧑‍💼 Persona: Clinic Manager
Story 4: Appointment Scheduling Control

As a Clinic Manager, I want to manage doctor schedules and appointment slots, so that clinic flow remains balanced.

Acceptance Criteria

Configure working hours per doctor

Define appointment durations

Block unavailable periods

Prevent double booking

Priority: High
Module: Appointments & Scheduling

Story 5: No-Show Management

As a Clinic Manager, I want to track patient no-shows, so that operational losses are reduced.

Acceptance Criteria

Mark no-show visits

Generate no-show reports

Trigger follow-up reminders

Priority: Medium
Module: CRM & Communication

Story 6: Daily Clinic Operations

As a Clinic Manager, I want to open and close clinic operational days, so that daily performance is tracked accurately.

Acceptance Criteria

Open day initializes counters

Close day locks data

Generates end-of-day report

Priority: High
Module: Operations Management

🧾 Persona: Receptionist
Story 7: Patient Registration

As a Receptionist, I want to register new patients quickly, so that waiting time is minimized.

Acceptance Criteria

Mandatory fields validation

Duplicate patient detection

Auto-assign patient ID

Priority: High
Module: Patient Management

Story 8: Appointment Booking

As a Receptionist, I want to book appointments based on doctor availability, so that scheduling conflicts are avoided.

Acceptance Criteria

Visual calendar view

Real-time availability

Confirmation sent to patient

Priority: High
Module: Appointments & Scheduling

Story 9: Visit Check-In

As a Receptionist, I want to mark patient arrival, so that doctors are notified automatically.

Acceptance Criteria

One-click check-in

Status visible to doctor

Timestamp recorded

Priority: Medium
Module: Front Desk Operations

🧑‍⚕️ Persona: Doctor
Story 10: Access Patient Medical History

As a Doctor, I want to access full patient medical history, so that diagnosis is accurate.

Acceptance Criteria

View previous diagnoses

View medications and test results

Read-only access to past visits

Priority: High
Module: Medical Records

Story 11: Record Consultation Details

As a Doctor, I want to document diagnosis and treatment during the visit, so that continuity of care is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria

Structured medical form

Attach files (labs, scans)

Save draft and finalize

Priority: High
Module: Medical Records

Story 12: Follow-Up Scheduling

As a Doctor, I want to recommend follow-up visits, so that treatment plans are enforced.

Acceptance Criteria

Suggest follow-up date

Visible to receptionist

Optional reminder trigger

Priority: Medium
Module: Appointments

💰 Persona: Accountant
Story 13: Generate Patient Invoice

As an Accountant, I want to generate invoices based on services rendered, so that billing is accurate.

Acceptance Criteria

Auto-fetch services from visit

Apply pricing rules

Issue invoice number

Priority: High
Module: Billing & Finance

Story 14: Payment Tracking

As an Accountant, I want to record payments and outstanding balances, so that financial records remain accurate.

Acceptance Criteria

Support partial payments

Track outstanding amounts

Payment method logging

Priority: High
Module: Billing & Finance

Story 15: Financial Reporting

As an Accountant, I want to generate financial reports, so that clinic profitability is transparent.

Acceptance Criteria

Daily / monthly reports

Export to Excel/PDF

Filter by service or doctor

Priority: Medium
Module: Finance & Reporting
