## 1. Executive Summary

### 1.1 Vision Statement
Bassan.os is envisioned as a **foundational operating system for modern service-driven businesses**, designed to unify operations, decision-making, accountability, and growth within a single, adaptable SaaS platform.

The platform serves as a **business control layer** that enables organizations to:
- Structure themselves correctly from day one
- Evolve internal workflows without system replacement
- Scale teams, departments, and revenue models without operational collapse
- Maintain clarity, ownership, and traceability across all business activities

### 1.2 Business Problem Statement
Many companies across medical, educational, marketing, service, and product-based industries operate with fragmented systems, manual workflows, disconnected teams, and tools that are not aligned with real-world business operations.

#### Key Market Problems:
1. **Fragmentation of Business Operations**
   - Disparate systems for CRM, accounting, commissions, communication
   - Loss of accountability and inconsistent data
   - No single source of truth
   - High operational overhead and management blind spots

2. **Lack of Structural Flexibility**
   - Existing systems assume fixed department structures
   - Rigid workflows and one-size-fits-all processes
   - Systems break when business changes

3. **Poor Visibility for Management**
   - Executives struggle to answer critical questions about ownership, status, and accountability
   - Decisions are reactive rather than strategic

4. **Absence of Client Transparency**
   - Clients lack visibility into service progress
   - Increased support load and reduced trust

### 1.3 Business Opportunity
Bassan.os addresses a clear market opportunity in the regional B2B SaaS market by providing:
- Horizontal platform for multiple industries
- Operating model as a product
- Regional gap in enterprise-grade SaaS

### 1.4 Strategic Alignment
Bassan.os supports growth across four dimensions:
1. Revenue Growth
2. Organizational Growth
3. Operational Maturity
4. Geographic Expansion

## 5. Business Requirements Catalog

### 5.1 Organizational Governance Requirements

#### BR-01: Multi-Organization Governance Support
**Description**: The system must support multiple independent organizations operating in isolated environments, each with its own governance rules.

**Priority**: Must
**Business Value**: Enables SaaS scalability and data protection
**Impact if Missing**: Platform cannot operate as a true B2B SaaS
**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Organizations are logically and physically isolated
- Each organization has independent configuration
- Governance rules apply only within organization scope
- No cross-organization data leakage is possible
- Organization-level audit trails are maintained

#### BR-02: Role-Based Authority Definition
**Description**: Organizations must be able to define roles with explicit authority scopes across departments and workflows.

**Priority**: Must
**Business Value**: Eliminates decision ambiguity
**Impact if Missing**: Governance collapse and operational conflict
**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Roles can be defined with granular permissions
- Authority scopes can be limited by department
- Role inheritance is supported
- Role changes are audited
- Role conflicts are detected and prevented

#### BR-03: Delegated Decision-Making Control
**Description**: Authority must be delegable and revocable without restructuring the organization.

**Priority**: Must
**Business Value**: Supports growth and flexibility
**Impact if Missing**: Central bottlenecks and leadership overload
**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Authority can be delegated to other users
- Delegation can be time-limited
- Delegation can be revoked immediately
- Delegation history is maintained
- Delegation rules can be configured

### 5.2 Workflow & Process Orchestration Requirements

#### BR-04: Configurable Workflow Creation
**Description**: Organizations must define workflows reflecting real operational processes without system modification.

**Priority**: Must
**Business Value**: Operational adaptability
**Impact if Missing**: System rigidity and abandonment
**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Workflow builder provides drag-and-drop interface
- Workflows can be versioned and rolled back
- Workflow changes can be tested in sandbox environment
- Workflow changes can be scheduled for deployment
- Workflow execution can be monitored in real-time

#### BR-05: Conditional Routing Logic
**Description**: Workflows must route tasks based on business conditions (e.g., paid vs unpaid, new vs existing client).

**Priority**: Must
**Business Value**: Accurate process execution
**Impact if Missing**: Manual intervention overload
**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Conditional routing supports multiple criteria types
- Routing rules can be complex (AND/OR logic)
- Routing decisions are logged and auditable
- Conditional routing can be tested before deployment

#### BR-06: Exception & Escalation Handling
**Description**: Workflows must include exception paths and escalation rules.

**Priority**: Must
**Business Value**: Risk containment
**Impact if Missing**: Silent failures and service breakdowns
**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Exception paths can be defined for each workflow step
- Escalation rules can be configured based on time thresholds
- Escalation paths can be multi-level
- Exception handling can trigger notifications
- All exceptions are logged with context

### 5.3 Task, Ownership & Accountability Requirements

#### BR-07: Explicit Task Ownership
**Description**: Every task must have one accountable owner at any point in time.

**Priority**: Must
**Business Value**: Accountability enforcement
**Impact if Missing**: Blame-shifting culture
**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Tasks have single owner field
- Task ownership history is maintained
- Task ownership can be transferred with audit trail
- System prevents unassigned tasks in active workflows
- Task ownership is visible in all relevant views

#### BR-08: Evidence-Based Task Completion
**Description**: Task completion must be supported by verifiable evidence when required.

**Priority**: Must
**Business Value**: Auditability and trust
**Impact if Missing**: Disputes and credibility loss
**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Evidence requirements can be defined per task type
- Multiple evidence types supported (documents, images, links)
- Evidence can be validated by designated roles
- Evidence is linked to task completion
- Evidence history is maintained

#### BR-09: Performance Attribution
**Description**: Work completed must be attributable to individuals and departments.

**Priority**: Must
**Business Value**: Fair evaluation and compensation
**Impact if Missing**: Incentive disputes
**Acceptance Criteria**:
- All work completion is attributed to individuals
- Department attribution is calculated from individual attribution
- Attribution data is available for reporting
- Attribution rules can be configured
- Attribution history is maintained

### 5.4 Customer Lifecycle Management Requirements

#### BR-10: Customer State Classification
**Description**: Customers must be classified by lifecycle state (new, active, inactive, churned).

**Priority**: Must
**Business Value**: Journey clarity
**Impact if Missing**: Misaligned engagement
**Acceptance Criteria**:

**Acceptance Criteria**:

- Payment status can be synced from external systems

#### BR-12: Customer-to-Workflow Binding
**Description**: Customer actions must trigger workflows across departments.

**Priority**: Must
**Business Value**: End-to-end continuity
**Impact if Missing**: Fragmented customer experience
**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Customer actions can be configured as workflow triggers
- Workflow binding is visible in customer profiles
- Workflow execution history is linked to customers
- Cross-department workflows can be triggered by customer actions
- Workflow binding rules can be configured

## 7. Business Process Flows

### 7.1 Process Design Principles (Business-Level)
1. **Every process has an owner**
2. **Every step has an input and output**
3. **No handoff without accountability**
4. **No execution without visibility**
5. **Exceptions are first-class citizens**

### 7.2 High-Level End-to-End Business Flow

#### 7.2.1 Universal Business Flow (Macro View)
Opportunity / Need
↓
Customer Engagement
↓
Qualification & Validation
↓
Commercial Decision
↓
Service / Product Delivery
↓
Customer Support & Retention
↓
Performance & Financial Review

### 7.3 Customer Lifecycle Process Flows

#### 7.3.1 New Customer Flow
**Trigger**: Lead generated from any source

**Flow Narrative**:
1. Lead is registered in the system
2. Lead ownership is assigned
3. Qualification criteria are applied
4. Lead becomes qualified prospect OR disqualified lead
5. Qualified prospect enters commercial workflow
6. Decision: converted to paying customer / remains pipeline / lost

**Exception Scenarios**:
- Duplicate leads
- Unresponsive prospects

#### 7.3.2 Existing Customer Flow
**Trigger**: Returning customer interaction or repeat order

**Flow Narrative**:
1. Customer identified as existing
2. Historical data loaded
3. Service eligibility validated
4. Workflow routed based on payment status and service history
5. Service execution initiated

### 7.4 Sales-to-Operations Handoff Flow

#### 7.4.1 Sales Completion Flow
**Trigger**: Commercial agreement reached

**Flow Narrative**:
1. Sales marks opportunity as "Won"
2. System validates payment status and contract scope
3. Delivery workflow is instantiated
4. Operations team is notified
5. Ownership is transferred

**Critical Rule**: Sales responsibility ENDS at defined point.

### 7.5 Marketing-to-Sales Attribution Flow

#### 7.5.1 Campaign Attribution Flow
**Trigger**: Lead enters system from marketing source

**Flow Narrative**:
1. Campaign source captured
2. Lead assigned to sales
3. Conversion tracked
4. Revenue attributed back to campaign

**Exception Handling**: Multi-touch attribution conflicts

### 7.6 Operations / Service Delivery Flow

#### 7.6.1 Internal Delivery Flow
**Trigger**: Approved delivery task

**Flow Narrative**:
1. Task created with SLA
2. Owner assigned
3. Execution evidence collected
4. Quality check performed
5. Completion validated

#### 7.6.2 External Partner Delivery Flow
**Trigger**: Outsourced service activation

**Flow Narrative**:
1. Partner assigned
2. Scope defined
3. Milestones tracked
4. Deliverables validated by both parties
5. Completion logged

**Governance Rule**: Visibility ≠ Full Access

### 7.7 Customer Support & Issue Resolution Flow

**Trigger**: Customer issue reported

## 8. Non-Functional Business Requirements

### 8.1 Performance Expectations (Business Perspective)

#### 8.1.1 Business Responsiveness
**Requirement**: The platform must feel responsive enough to support real-time operational decision-making across departments.

**Business Meaning**:
- Managers must be able to act without delay
- Frontline staff must not experience friction during execution
- Delays translate directly into lost productivity and trust

**Business Impact if Not Met**:
- Shadow systems emerge (Excel, WhatsApp, phone calls)
- Users bypass the platform
- Data integrity collapses

#### 8.1.2 Workflow Execution Timeliness
**Requirement**: Business workflows must execute and transition states without perceived lag that disrupts operations.

**Business Meaning**:
- Task routing must not block service delivery
- Notifications must arrive while still relevant

**Business Impact if Not Met**:
- Missed SLAs
- Escalations increase
- System seen as "the problem," not the solution

### 8.2 Availability & Business Continuity

#### 8.2.1 Operational Availability
**Requirement**: Bassan.os must be available during core business hours across supported regions.

**Business Meaning**:
- Downtime equals operational paralysis
- Customers cannot "wait" for their operating system

**Business Impact if Not Met**:
- Immediate loss of trust
- Contractual disputes
- Revenue churn

#### 8.2.2 Business Continuity Assurance
**Requirement**: Organizations must trust that their operational data and workflows are protected against loss.

**Business Meaning**:
- Businesses rely on historical records for decisions and disputes
- Loss of data is equivalent to loss of institutional memory

**Business Impact if Not Met**:
- Legal exposure
- Irreversible client damage
- Market reputation collapse

### 8.3 Security & Compliance (Business Impact View)

#### 8.3.1 Data Isolation & Confidentiality
**Requirement**: Each organization's data must be strictly isolated from others.

**Business Meaning**:
- Customers must trust Bassan.os with sensitive operational data
- Any breach affects *all* customers, not just one

**Business Impact if Not Met**:
- Regulatory penalties
- Market exit in certain regions
- Loss of enterprise clients

#### 8.3.2 Role-Based Access Integrity
**Requirement**: Users must only see and act on what they are authorized to.

**Business Meaning**:
- Prevents internal misuse
- Protects against accidental damage

**Business Impact if Not Met**:
- Internal disputes
- Compliance violations
- Loss of governance credibility

#### 8.3.3 Regulatory Readiness
**Requirement**: The platform must be capable of supporting regional data protection and audit requirements.

**Business Meaning**:
- Customers operate in regulated environments
- Compliance is a buying decision factor

**Business Impact if Not Met**:
- Blocked enterprise deals
- Forced customer churn

### 8.4 Scalability from a Revenue & Growth Perspective

#### 8.4.1 Organizational Growth Scalability
**Requirement**: Customers must grow (users, departments, workflows) without operational degradation.

**Business Meaning**:
- Growth should not require system replacement
- Platform should grow *with* the business

**Business Impact if Not Met**:
- Customers outgrow the system
- High churn at growth stage

#### 8.4.2 Multi-Company Expansion
**Requirement**: The platform must support customers operating multiple legal entities.

**Business Meaning**:
- Holding companies and groups are common
- Fragmented systems destroy group-level oversight

**Business Impact if Not Met**:
- Lost enterprise opportunities
- Reduced contract value

### 8.5 Usability & Adoption (Business Adoption Lens)

#### 8.5.1 Business User Accessibility

**Business Meaning**:
- Adoption depends on perceived ease
- Training cost must be reasonable

**Business Impact if Not Met**:
- Low adoption

**Business Meaning**:

- Flexibility reduces adoption friction

**Business Impact if Not Met**:
- Resistance from teams
- Partial usage and failure

### 8.6 Observability, Auditability & Trust

**Business Impact if Not Met**:

| BR-01 | Customer adoption resistance due to workflow complexity | Medium | High | Provide configurable workflows, guided onboarding, progressive adoption modules |
| BR-02 | Misaligned departmental KPIs | High | Medium | Cross-department dashboards, KPI alignment sessions, automated attribution |
| BR-03 | Commission disputes for remote employees | Medium | High | Transparent tracking, evidence-based task ownership, automated reporting |
| BR-04 | Failure to deliver promised inter-company collaboration | Low | High | Clear SLA templates, milestone tracking, acknowledgment flows |
| BR-05 | Over-reliance on manual inputs leading to errors | High | Medium | Automated data capture, validation rules, exception management |
| BR-06 | Lack of visibility for executives | Medium | High | Executive dashboards, consolidated reporting, alerts for anomalies |

| Risk ID | Description | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|---------|------------|-----------|--------|-------------------|

| MR-02 | Market unfamiliarity with multi-tenant workflow orchestration | Medium | Medium | Market education campaigns, onboarding workshops, proof-of-concept pilots |
| MR-03 | Regulatory barriers in certain regions | Low | High | Preemptive compliance support, modular regulatory adherence features |

### 9.3 Legal & Compliance Risks

| Risk ID | Description | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|---------|------------|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| LR-01 | Data privacy breaches across multiple tenants | Low | Very High | Strong data isolation, encryption, strict RBAC, periodic audits |
| LR-02 | Non-compliance with regional labor or financial regulations | Medium | High | Regional customization, legal consultation, compliance templates |
| LR-03 | Disputes between partner companies | Medium | Medium | Contractual templates, milestone acknowledgments, audit trail evidence |

### 9.4 Operational Risks

| Risk ID | Description | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|---------|------------|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| OR-01 | Workflow misconfigurations causing operational failures | Medium | High | Workflow validation, sandbox testing, approval gates |
| OR-02 | Data entry errors leading to inaccurate reporting | High | Medium | Input validation, automated checks, error notification system |
| OR-03 | Task overload for staff due to poor distribution | Medium | Medium | Automated task allocation, resource load balancing, alerts for bottlenecks |
| OR-04 | Low adoption by remote workforce | Medium | High | Remote-friendly UX, integration with LinkedIn profiles, clear performance tracking |

   - Escalation protocols

   - Early pilot deployments
   - Clear ROI messaging

4. **Compliance & Legal Risk Mitigation**
   - RBAC and tenant isolation
   - Regional regulatory templates
   - Audit logs and traceability

   - Transparent commission calculation
   - Cross-department KPI alignment
   - Automated reporting for payroll and billing
